Our History

Two Decades of Powering Industrial Progress

Founded in 2006, TBM Power has grown from a specialized relay testing outfit into a full-scale industrial electrical engineering firm. We design, build, and maintain high-voltage infrastructure across North America and global export markets.

Our engineering ethos centers around fault resistance, energetic sustainability, and rapid deployment. We partner with heavy manufacturers, utility grids, pharmaceutical hubs, and chemical refineries to keep their critical power systems running without interruption.

With over 150 commissioned high-voltage projects, our engineers bring hands-on field experience combined with advanced software modeling to resolve complex electrical challenges.
