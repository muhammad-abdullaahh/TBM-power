Residential Solar Installation — DHA Phase 6, Karachi

Renewable Energy • System Size: 10 kW (On-Grid Hybrid System)

TBM Power completed a 10kW hybrid solar installation for a private residence in DHA Phase 6, Karachi, engineered to significantly reduce dependence on grid electricity while maintaining seamless backup during load-shedding hours. The system was designed after a detailed load audit of the homeowner's monthly consumption pattern, ensuring optimal panel sizing and inverter capacity for year-round performance.

The installation includes high-efficiency monocrystalline solar panels mounted on a corrosion-resistant aluminum frame, a hybrid inverter capable of automatic grid-to-battery switching, and a battery storage bank sized to cover essential household loads during outages. Our team handled the complete project lifecycle — from site survey and structural assessment to net-metering coordination with K-Electric, wiring, panel installation, and final commissioning.

Since going live, the system has reduced the homeowner's monthly electricity bill by an estimated 85%, with excess units exported back to the grid under the net-metering agreement. The project reflects TBM Power's commitment to delivering reliable, future-proof energy solutions tailored to real household consumption needs.

Residential Solar Installation — Korangi, Karachi

Renewable Energy • System Size: 3 kW (On-Grid Residential System)

TBM Power completed a compact 3kW on-grid solar installation for a residential home in Korangi, Karachi, designed to help the homeowner manage rising electricity costs on a modest household budget. The project was scoped as a straightforward, cost-effective entry-level solar solution — ideal for smaller homes with moderate daily power consumption such as lighting, fans, refrigeration, and a small AC unit.

Our team carried out a quick site assessment to determine optimal panel placement on the home's rooftop, followed by installation of standard-efficiency solar panels paired with a compact grid-tied inverter. The setup required minimal structural modification, allowing for a fast turnaround from site survey to full commissioning within just a few days.

Since installation, the homeowner has seen a noticeable drop in their monthly K-Electric bill, with the system efficiently covering daytime household loads. This project highlights TBM Power's ability to deliver reliable, affordable solar solutions for everyday residential clients — not just large-scale commercial installations.

Solar Power System & Street Lighting — Gulshan-e-Maymar Housing Society, Karachi

Renewable Energy • System Size: 150 kW (On-Grid Community System) + Solar Street Lighting Network

TBM Power executed a large-scale dual-scope solar project for a residential housing society in Gulshan-e-Maymar, Karachi, combining a 150kW community-level solar power system with a fully independent solar street lighting network across the society's internal roads. The project was designed to serve the society's shared amenities — clubhouse, community center, water pumping stations, and common area lighting — while separately addressing public safety through reliable, off-grid street illumination.

For the main power system, our engineering team conducted a comprehensive site survey across the society's rooftops and open utility areas, followed by installation of high-output solar panels mounted on custom-engineered ground-mount and rooftop structures, paired with commercial-grade inverters sized for the society's peak daytime demand. In parallel, TBM Power's team installed a network of standalone solar street lamps — each unit equipped with an integrated panel, battery, and LED fixture — replacing the society's traditional grid-powered street lighting and eliminating reliance on grid electricity for nighttime road safety.

Industrial Solar Power System — Manufacturing Facility, Malir Industrial Area, Karachi

Renewable Energy • System Size: 500 kW (On-Grid Commercial/Industrial System)

TBM Power delivered a high-capacity 500kW solar power system for a manufacturing facility in the Malir Industrial Area, engineered to offset a significant portion of the plant's heavy daytime power consumption from production machinery, industrial motors, compressors, and factory-wide lighting. Given the scale and criticality of continuous industrial operations, the project demanded rigorous planning around load compatibility, power quality, and zero-downtime integration with the facility's existing electrical infrastructure.

Our engineering team began with a detailed industrial load audit and rooftop structural assessment across the factory's extensive shed-type roofing, followed by installation of high-efficiency solar panels mounted on custom steel racking designed for industrial rooftop profiles. The system was paired with commercial-grade string inverters and synchronized with the facility's existing switchgear to ensure seamless grid-solar interoperability without interrupting production schedules. TBM Power also handled load balancing calculations to ensure the solar output aligned with the facility's peak operational hours, maximizing self-consumption before any grid export.

Since commissioning, the facility has achieved a substantial reduction in its industrial electricity costs, improving overall production economics and supporting the client's sustainability goals. The project reinforces TBM Power's expertise in delivering robust, industrial-grade solar solutions built to withstand the demands of continuous manufacturing environments.

Commercial Solar Power System — Industrial Unit, Gulistan-e-Johar, Karachi

Renewable Energy • System Size: 75 kW (On-Grid Commercial System)

TBM Power completed a 75kW commercial solar installation for an industrial unit located in Gulistan-e-Johar, Karachi, aimed at reducing the facility's operational electricity expenses driven by production equipment, storage refrigeration, and extended working-hour lighting loads. The project was scoped to fit within the constraints of a mid-sized industrial premises, balancing available rooftop space with the client's actual daytime energy demand.

Our team conducted a site assessment covering rooftop orientation, shading from adjacent structures, and the facility's electrical load profile, then designed a system using commercial-grade solar panels mounted on a reinforced rooftop racking system suited to the building's structural layout. A centralized inverter setup was installed and synchronized with the unit's existing power distribution board, allowing for smooth grid-solar integration without disrupting ongoing business operations during commissioning.

Since going operational, the facility has seen a meaningful drop in its monthly electricity costs, with the solar system covering a strong share of daytime operational load. This project reflects TBM Power's continued track record of delivering dependable, right-sized commercial solar solutions across Karachi's diverse industrial and commercial zones.
